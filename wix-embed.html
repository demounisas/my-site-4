What is Company Management Software?

A shared workspace brings finance, projects, sales, and people onto one record, so every department reads from the same live numbers — the core idea behind company management software. Teams stop juggling separate spreadsheets and standalone apps, and get a single, connected system built to plan, track, and report on daily operations, running directly on top of your existing Odoo setup. A sales rep, a project manager, and a finance lead all pull numbers from the identical dataset, so nobody double-checks whose spreadsheet is current.
