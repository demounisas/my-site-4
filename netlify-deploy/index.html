TYPES OF SOFTWARE

Types of Business Management Software

Not every business runs on a single tool. Most companies combine several types of business management software — the ones that matter most are below.

Project Management Software

Plan, assign, and track project tasks from one shared view.

Customer Relationship Management (CRM) Software

A customer management system for small business teams tracking every deal.

Financial Management Software

Manage accounting, invoicing, and financial reporting together in real time.

Human Resources Management Software (HRMS)

Handle hiring, onboarding, and employee records in one central place.

Collaboration and Communication Software

Keep comments, approvals, and updates visible to the whole team.

Document Management Software

Business document management software to store, version, and secure files.

Customer Support and Help Desk Software

Resolve tickets and track response times from one shared queue.

Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) Software

Run core business operations — accounting, inventory, records — from one system.
