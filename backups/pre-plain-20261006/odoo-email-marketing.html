    MAILINGS OR AUTOMATION

    When Should Email Marketing Be Combined With Odoo Marketing Automation?

    Use a mailing when the same message goes to a list at one moment. Add Marketing Automation when timing depends on each person: when they joined, opened, clicked or ordered. Make your call for each campaign.

    4. Resend the welcome email with a new subject to people who didn’t open it
